Core Commitments

Pillars of Our Civic Vision

Grounded in historic sacrifice and forward-looking reform, our program centers on citizens' rights and institutional rule of law.

  • Equal Citizenship: Full civil liberties, free expression, and equal protection under the law across all regions.
  • Inclusive Economy: Fair job creation, agricultural modernization, energy security, and transparent public finance.
  • Lasting Peace: Inclusive national dialogue, transitional justice, and independent democratic institutions.
